# Research Publications

## Design and Simulation of a Wearable Body Area Antenna Using CST Software with Cloud-Based Data Storage

Published in the International Journal of Multidisciplinary Research in Science, Engineering and Technology (IJMRSET), Volume 9, Issue 4, April 2026.

The research focuses on the design and simulation of a wearable Body Area Antenna using CST Software for healthcare applications, with cloud-based data storage.
